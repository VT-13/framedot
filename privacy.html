Privacy.

FrameDot is operated by Vihaan Tirumala. Questions or requests about your data: vihaan.tirumala@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, the time you opted in, and the version of this notice shown to you. We count each email address once. We do not ask for your name or payment details.

To protect the form against abuse, we also keep a short-lived record of accepted submissions: the time, a challenge identifier, and keyed hashes derived from the submitting network address and email. We do not store your raw IP address in our waitlist database. Our hosting providers may process technical request data needed to serve and secure the site.

Why we use it

We use your email only to manage FrameDot early access and send updates about availability if you opt in. Consent is our basis for those emails; you can withdraw it at any time. We use short-lived submission records to prevent replay and excessive submissions and to keep the signup count useful.

Who handles it

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The waitlist API and database run on Cloudflare through Sites. These providers process data to deliver and secure the service. We do not sell your email or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We remove waitlist email records after 24 months, or sooner if you ask us to delete yours. Abuse-prevention submission records are removed after 30 days. Expired records are cleared when the waitlist service next receives a request.

Your choices

To access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for your email, write to vihaan.tirumala@gmail.com. We will verify the request before changing the record. Any early-access email we send will also provide a way to unsubscribe. Depending on where you live, you may have additional data rights or the right to complain to a privacy regulator.

Provider infrastructure may process data outside your country. Contact us if you need more detail about where your data is handled.
